I finished it about 2 months ago but did not take the time to review it
it was a good book. It introduces a new character called Eustace which is a nasty
little boy and that sums up a substantial part of the book. The history focuses
on a trip that the now king Prince Caspian takes looking for some of his subordinates.
It's full of jokes and more context on some of the characters from the [last book](https://kengru.do/narnia-4).

The book closes with the kids returning home as always and we say goodbye to a character
that has a lot of that, character.
